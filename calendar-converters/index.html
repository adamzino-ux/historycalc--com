Historical Date & Calendar Converters

A date in an old document depends on the calendar it was written in. These converters move dates between the Gregorian, Julian, Hebrew, Islamic Hijri, French Republican and Mayan Long Count calendars. They also find the weekday of any historical date, decode the Aztec Sun Stone, look up the Chinese zodiac sign for a birth year, work out how old someone was at a historical event, and compare Roman emperors' reigns. Each page explains the rules behind the conversion, including leap-year differences.
